# What to ask Masbate tourism

Generated by `bin/rails last_trips:gaps` on 2026-09-20. Regenerate it as
answers come in.

**The question, every time, is the same: what time does the last one leave,
coming back?** Not the first one, and not the outbound direction — the two
ends of a route rarely run to the same clock, and it is the return that
strands people. The last pumpboat off Masbate City is 13:00 and the last one
off Ticao is 14:00, and that hour is the whole difference.

Useful to say why: this is for an offline travel guide, the figures are
credited to the source, and a wrong one is worse than a blank.

21 unanswered, ordered by how many destinations each one blocks.
Answers go in `data/last_trips.csv`, one row per leg per boarding stop.

## Also worth confirming while you have them

- **`L_RORO_MOBO_SANJACINTO`** — MARINA certifies Mobo to San Jacinto as a
  served route and publishes no times for it. It is the only Masbate-island
  link to Ticao that skips Masbate City, and the app now routes Ticao–Burias
  over it, so a timetable here matters more than its traffic suggests.
- **Aroroy to Bugui Point** — the ₱50 public boat and the ₱800 charter are
  both January 2018 figures.
- **The Bulan–San Jacinto sailing times** — the 2017 and 2026 sources
  disagree, and MARINA records San Jacinto as daytime navigation only.
- **Whether the Mobo–Claveria RoRo really leaves at 00:01.** It chains
  correctly with the Pio Duran leg, but it is an odd enough hour to be worth
  one confirmation.
